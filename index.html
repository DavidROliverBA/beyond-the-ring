An unofficial, non-commercial fan project inspired by The Expanse, the creation of James S. A. Corey. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the authors, their publishers or the makers of the television series. Planets, moons, ships and stations are drawn to true size and distance, so most of space is empty; ships from across the story are all on show at once.
